MoviesAura V12 — Clickable Card Foundation

Base: V11

Applied:
- Movie, Web Series and TV Show cards have a reusable click-navigation foundation.
- The title inside each card follows the same destination as the card.
- Existing V11 visual design is preserved.
- Firebase integration can later populate each card's data-detail-url with the
  Firebase document-specific detail/download page.

Example:
data-detail-url="movie.html?id=FIREBASE_DOCUMENT_ID"

When Firebase rendering is implemented, call:
window.MoviesAuraCardNavigation.refresh();
after new cards are inserted into the DOM.
